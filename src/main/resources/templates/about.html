About VoiceLink

Understand each other, one turn at a time.

VoiceLink helps two people follow short spoken turns with live captions and translated voice.

How VoiceLink works

Create a private two-person room and share its invite code, or join with a code you received. Each person speaks English or Tamil and receives the other language.

In an active room, record a short push-to-talk turn. Speech recognition produces a source caption, translation produces the other-language caption, and generated voice can be played back by room members.

Privacy & Data

During development, use only non-sensitive, non-confidential conversation content. Speech is sent to Groq for recognition. The current utterance, bounded recent context, and matching room glossary terms may be sent to Gemini for translation; translated text is sent to Gemini for voice generation.

Recent captions, bounded conversation context, and generated audio are temporary. VoiceLink does not store a persistent transcript history. Context is held in memory and cleared when the room closes or the application restarts.

Room glossary entries are stored in PostgreSQL while a room is open. They survive reconnects and application restarts, and are deleted when the room is explicitly closed.

AI Service Profiles

Standard

Groq handles speech recognition; Gemini handles translation and generated voice. Standard uses free-tier services with usage quotas when the server's free-tier configuration is confirmed.

Premium

Premium shares Groq speech recognition and uses a separate paid Gemini configuration for translation and voice. There is no automatic fallback between profiles.

Premium is not available on this server.

The room creator chooses a profile when creating the room. That choice cannot be changed later.

Current Scope

VoiceLink supports two-person English ↔ Tamil rooms and short, push-to-talk speech turns. Recent captions help members follow the active conversation; they are not a permanent chat history.

Rooms can keep a small, room-specific glossary until they are explicitly closed.
